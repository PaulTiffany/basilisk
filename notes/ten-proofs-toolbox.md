# Ten-proofs toolbox audit

This note does **not** claim that OpenAI's ten mathematical results form one Lipschitz theory. The purpose is to identify proof techniques that may strengthen the quartet if they are not already present in Principia Symbolica.

## Highest-priority import: auxiliary certificates and transform duality

The sphere-packing work uses an auxiliary object whose local sign constraints become a global certificate after transform-domain analysis. The quartet analogue is a dual presentation of boundary admissibility:

\[
\text{local I/O inequalities}
\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{global dual certificate}.
\]

Research question: for which finite or harmonic contract classes does there exist a transform \(\mathcal T\) and auxiliary function \(f\) such that

\[
f\in\mathcal A(C)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathcal T f\in\mathcal D(C),
\]

with soundness in both directions under explicit hypotheses?

This is directly relevant to the movement:

\[
\text{Blanket observations}
\rightarrow
\text{Script constraints}
\rightarrow
\text{Contract certificate}.
\]

## Other candidate tools

- **Representation transition graphs:** possible spectral certificates for coherent blanket mediation.
- **Local-to-global obstruction / non-soficity:** useful for showing that locally compliant I/O behavior need not arise from a globally coherent Script.
- **Non-identifiability of generators:** supports the separation of Script from Ledger.
- **Critical-locus and transcendence-degree lower bounds:** candidate realization-cost lower bounds for Scripts.
- **Parallel repetition under correlated strategies:** possible amplification theorem for adaptive boundary testing.
- **Moment reconstruction:** candidate compressed Ledger with algebraic decoding guarantees.
- **Finite-family extremal gaps:** exact language for collective Contract restriction exceeding every individual clause.

Each import must pass three tests:

1. It states a new lemma in quartet vocabulary.
2. It does not merely rename an existing PS theorem.
3. Its assumptions are explicit enough to permit a counterexample.
