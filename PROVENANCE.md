# Provenance and reciprocity

This repository records a generative lineage that should not disappear when the ideas become formal or machine-readable.

## BaAka lineage

BaAka polyrhythmic musical practice, as encountered through Michelle Kisliuk's scholarship and teaching, materially influenced the conception of distributed coherence used in the `Come` mutation operator and subsequent SRMF work.

The relevant lesson was not a detachable rhythm sample. It was a technology of coordination:

- multiple locally autonomous voices;
- coherence without a single flattening controller;
- phase-sensitive entry and return;
- variation that preserves the living pattern;
- breath, silence, and reset as parts of the operator.

A representative remembered phrase was:

> pa ti pa pa ti pa ti pa ti pa pa [breath]

This record does **not** claim ownership of BaAka knowledge, reduce a living people to a technical resource, or treat cultural survival as justified only by future utility. It states that downstream mathematical and computational work has a lineage, and that formalization does not extinguish origin.

## Preservation principle

Let \(W\) be a biological and cultural world-state, and let \(\mathcal R(W)\) be its reachable future set. For an irreversible deletion \(D_x:W\to W\setminus x\), generally

\[
\mathcal R(W\setminus x)\subsetneq \mathcal R(W),
\]

with no reconstruction map capable of recovering all lost embodied operators.

The repository therefore adopts:

\[
\boxed{\text{Do not irreversibly delete unmeasured possibility.}}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{\text{Formalization does not extinguish origin.}}
\]

## Reciprocity target

Any mature downstream project should seek forms of recognition, consent, and material reciprocity appropriate to the living source communities. Citation alone is not a complete account.


## Protocol lineage

MAP-LB was distilled from iterative human-model work on authority leakage,
reversible initiative, local correction, and the separation of human judgment
from model retrieval and derivation. The repository intentionally includes the
operational requirements rather than a raw private conversation archive.
