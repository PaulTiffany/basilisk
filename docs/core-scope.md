# Finite core and consolidation policy

Basilisk is now intentionally operated as three nested research layers rather than one indefinitely expanding theorem surface.

\[
\boxed{\text{Basilisk Core} \subset \text{Parameterized Transformation Theory} \subset \text{Research Bridges}}
\]

The purpose of this split is not to rank ideas by importance. It is to preserve a finite engineering object whose assurance exterior can grow much larger than its conceptual interior.

## 1. Basilisk Core

The Core contains the distinctions required to state, execute, inspect, and adversarially test the present Basilisk Quartet + MAP-LB protocol:

- Contract / Script / Blanket / Ledger non-collapse;
- action gates and semantic boundary crossings;
- current-turn versus standing authority;
- permission breadth versus authorization freshness;
- judgment/promotion boundaries relevant to operative authority;
- Ledger integrity versus semantic truth;
- dependency-cut and controller interaction witnesses;
- provenance, formal closure, mutation pressure, and cross-witness interpretability;
- the boundary-aware Lipschitz theorem program.

A new claim belongs in Core only when at least one of the following is true:

1. hardening exposes a state or distinction the present Core cannot represent;
2. an existing Core claim cannot be stated or tested without it;
3. the human holder of the living surface explicitly expands Core scope.

Conceptual resonance alone is not sufficient reason to enlarge Core.

## 2. Parameterized Transformation Theory

This layer contains reusable mathematics that explains recurring structure without changing the operative controller contract. Current examples include:

- weighted pseudometric structure;
- frame-indexed staging and zero-kernel refinement;
- positive-scale invariance of indistinguishability;
- reflective-history parameterization of an operational arrow;
- amortization as parameter transport.

The intended development rule is: prove transport/invariance machinery once, then instantiate it rather than rebuilding the same geometry separately for each domain.

A Theory theorem may illuminate Core without becoming a Core requirement.

## 3. Research Bridges

Research Bridges connect the finite kernel to larger projects or domains. Current bridges include:

- richer physical/social materiality;
- probabilistic or Fristonian Markov-blanket semantics beyond the finite dependency cut;
- Principia Symbolica Born-arrow / dual-horizon / physical-time transport.

Bridge work must preserve explicit assumptions and non-collapse boundaries. A bridge does not become Core merely because the same meta-theorem appears there.

## Consolidation rule

During consolidation, the preferred change is to make the exterior denser rather than the center larger:

\[
\text{freeze} \to \text{enumerate} \to \text{mutate} \to \text{cross-check} \to \text{reproduce} \to \text{document}.
\]

The implementation may remain small while tests, generated fixtures, proof inventories, mutation campaigns, CI definitions, and evidence artifacts grow by orders of magnitude.

Generated assurance volume is preferable to hand-maintained duplication. Durable hand-written machinery should concentrate in specifications, generators, oracles, invariants, and mutation operators.

## Current prioritization

`verification/scope_registry.json` is the machine-readable source for current claim/frontier placement and open-debt priority.

In particular, universal Python/Lean controller correspondence (CF-001) remains a real Core debt but is presently deferred while external compiler/language infrastructure is being improved. Deferral changes scheduling, not truth status: the item remains open until its declared closure condition is met.

Research Bridges are presently parked during consolidation unless a bridge exposes a concrete missing Core distinction.

## What this policy does not mean

- It does not freeze the repository permanently.
- It does not demote Theory or Bridge work as intellectually secondary.
- It does not certify the current Core as complete, safe, aligned, or production-ready.
- It does not permit open debts to be relabeled as solved merely to obtain a finite scope.

The aim is narrower: maintain a finite, interpretable research kernel and let assurance grow around it without accidental conceptual sprawl.
